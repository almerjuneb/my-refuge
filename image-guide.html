A visual checklist

Images with care
and purpose.

Use real, respectful community photography. Choose images that show children as people with agency, not as symbols of hardship.

01 / Home hero

One welcoming human moment

Placement: Large right-hand image beside the main headline.

Shape: Landscape, approximately 4:3. Keep faces near the center for mobile crops.

Choose: A genuine, warm portrait or shared activity; natural light and direct eye contact.

Alt text: Describe who is pictured and what is happening, without guessing names or location.

02 / Who we are

Learning in action

Placement: Tall image beside the mission copy.

Shape: Portrait or square crop. Leave a little space around the people in frame.

Choose: A classroom, tutoring, reading, or school-supply moment that reflects actual programs.

Alt text: Explain the learning activity shown; avoid identifying children.

03 / Support us

People helping together

Placement: Wide supporting image beside the volunteer and fundraising introduction.

Shape: Landscape 3:2. Choose a crop that still works at half-width on a phone.

Choose: Volunteers preparing, organizing, or taking part in a community activity.

Alt text: Say what the volunteers are doing, not just that it is a photo.

04 / News & stories

A real moment from the story

Placement: Large feature image beside the story headline.

Shape: Landscape 3:2; allow room for a clear subject and avoid text over the image.

Choose: An approved photo from the actual event whenever possible; use this stock image only as a temporary stand-in.

Alt text: Describe the visible moment without implying it documents a specific My Refuge activity.

05 / Members & groups

Belonging, not stock profiles

Placement: Wide banner for Members; landscape group image for Groups.

Shape: Wide 16:9 banner or 3:2 card. Keep important faces inside the center 60%.

Choose: A consented photo of a real gathering or a neutral image of people connecting. Do not use stock faces as named members.

Alt text: Describe the gathering; don't suggest the pictured people are members if they aren't.

06 / Events

Show what people can join

Placement: Small event card image or wide section banner.

Shape: Landscape 16:9 for event cards, with no essential text baked into the image.

Choose: A real event photo after it has happened, or a welcoming activity image for future listings.

Alt text: Name the activity when known; don't imply a stock photo is a My Refuge event.

Before publishing

Make the image work for everyone.

  • Replace stock photos with consented My Refuge photography wherever available.
  • Confirm rights, image credits, and permissions before publishing. Unsplash image links here are visual placeholders, not a substitute for checking the selected photo's current license.
  • Upload optimized JPG or WebP files to Wix Media Manager; keep originals separately.
  • Add meaningful alt text, use consistent crops, and check contrast wherever text sits near a photo.
  • Use Wix image elements, galleries, or Events imagery. Keep text and calls to action as editable Wix text and buttons, not baked into images.

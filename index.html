Who we are

Care that opens
doors to the future.

At My Refuge, we believe every child deserves a chance to thrive, whatever their circumstances.

Our mission

Help children feel nourished, supported, and ready to learn.

My Refuge works to combat hunger and support quality education for underprivileged children in the Philippines. Alongside community partners and dedicated volunteers, we provide food assistance and help connect children with learning opportunities.

Our support includes nutritious meals, school supplies, tutoring, and mentorship. By responding to immediate needs while encouraging long-term educational growth, we hope to help young people dream bigger and reach their potential.

We are committed to a supportive community where every young person can grow, build resilience, and shape a brighter future.
